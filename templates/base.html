Configure AI Provider Key

To enable AI Smart Notes, ChromaDB Semantic Search, Flashcards, and RAG Chatbot, configure your AI provider key:

  1. Open the .env file in your project directory.
  2. Update one of:
    GROQ_API_KEY=gsk-your-key-here (default, free),
    OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-your-key-here, or
    GEMINI_API_KEY=your-key-here
  3. Restart your Flask application.
Default provider: Groq (free). Swap by changing LLM_PROVIDER in .env.
